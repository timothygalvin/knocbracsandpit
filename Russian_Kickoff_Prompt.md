Course Creation Playbook and CLAUDE.md in this project's files — same process

**Step 0 profile:**
- LEARNER: Timothy — adult, self-directed, technically comfortable, happy to
  be pushed. No accessibility accommodations needed.
- DOMAIN: Russian, restarting after a gap. Assume nothing about my retained
  level — that's the diagnostic's job. Probe from absolute basics (Cyrillic

- GOAL: get to a solid B2 proficiency, pushing into A1.  Be in a position to work in a Russian speaking organisation.
- TIME BUDGET: 15 mins every day and 1 hour at the weekend.
- THEME: propose 2–3 theme options for the course framing and let me pick

  something befitting my actual motivation, not "Unit 1: Greetings").

   schema per CLAUDE.md, grown-up styling, save/resume, results submitting

   Cyrillic reading/recognition, core vocabulary, grammar (cases and verb
   aspect deserve their own ramp), reading comprehension, and short
   writing/translation. Ramp each strand A0 → B1. Include the enjoyment
   ratings and survey questions — they matter for adults too.

   done the written one: a graduated conversation in Russian from greetings
   upward, pushing until I break, then a debrief in English. Design the
   protocol when you build the written assessment so the two halves cover
   complementary ground.
3. After both halves: the findings summary, the course plan document

   **Platform integration:** the course is a new folder + registry entry per
   CLAUDE.md — propose the course id. Respect every invariant in that file,
   especially PHP 5.6 on the server and the session-id/no-hyphens rule.
