## Findings

1. **Placement: solid A2, B1+ reading, communicative-A2 production.**
   Script 10/10. Reading: every Dima question right including the
   B1+ line. Production: both free-writing tasks communicate
   successfully with rough accuracy — Dima would understand
   everything, including the joke.
2. **Spontaneous grammar beats elicited grammar** (headline). He
   failed «новой работе» when asked cold, then wrote «на русском
   языке» (same construction, correct) unprompted; wrote present-tense
   «я живу» when asked for "I lived", then wrote «Я жил в Москве два
   года» perfectly in free text. His Russian lives in CHUNKS. The
   course must teach through chunks toward rules, not rules toward
   chunks.
3. **Self-report vs measurement disagree — the rubric's key split.**
   He says TENSES broke him; measurement says the aspect concept is
   INTACT (both forced choices right) and past tense is available in
   chunks — while CASES break immediately after prepositional (acc
   direction, animate acc, нет+gen, dative, instrumental all down;
   only chunk survivals: бутылка вина, много людей). Tenses = partly
   a confidence problem; cases = the real structural gap.
4. **Three small lexical bugs, cheap to fix, each hit twice or in a
   load-bearing spot:** друг↔другой (twice), заказать↔закончить,
   разговор/речь blur. Plus one register note: his «чек, пожалуйста»
   is real street Russian — credited; счёт is worth adding, not
   correcting.
5. **The harvest**: Sasha and the Schwartzkeiser ("drunken
   conversations… we understood each other. Russian is a language you
   have to feel") — the course now has its bar and its tone. Targets
   (v8q5): geopolitics · asking people about themselves AND
   understanding the answers · jokes and stories. All three are
   listening-heavy. Writing rated 5/5 (most fun despite hardest);
   verbs 2/5 (smuggle, don't drill); cases 3/5 (direct assault
   permitted).

## Architecture

10 weeks · 5 units. Rhythm fitted to 15 min/day + 1 h weekend:
each platform session is built in 2–3 save/resume sittings across
weekdays; the weekend hour alternates between the long session and
LIVE conversation practice (the oral-protocol prompt, reused as an
ongoing conversation partner — the protocol doubles as training).
Checkpoints cp1/cp2 after Units 2 and 4. **Listening workstream runs
beside the platform from week 1**: 10 min/day comprehensible input
(graded Russian podcast/YouTube at A2), named in every summit; the
platform cannot carry this and must not pretend to.

## Units

- **Unit 1 · The Courtyard** (знакомство — his emphasis #1).
  Introductions done properly: меня зовут / из Ирландии (из+gen) /
  **работаю архитектором** (rematch of v4q4.b, labelled: "this exact
  ending got away from you on the walk") / жил в Москве два года
  (his own correct sentence, promoted to canon). Plant the
  знакомиться family (his one blank). Kill the друг/другой bug in a
  reveal. Weekend: oral protocol run = baseline + first conversation.
- **Unit 2 · The Case Files** (direct assault — joy 3 permits, and
  he must see the enemy named). One case per session, each opening
  with a chunk he ALREADY owns (бутылка вина, много людей) and
  extracting the rule from it — chunk → rule, per finding 2. Session
  order: acc direction vs location (his exact «баре» bug, one
  reveal), нет+gen, dative of calling/giving, instrumental of
  profession (re-drilled). Rematches: every v4 item.
- **cp1 · Checkpoint: Papers, Please** — cases + Unit 1, plus the
  B1+ seeds (пять друзей, новой работе).
- **Unit 3 · Friday at the Schwartzkeiser** (the bar, rebuilt from
  his memory). заказать vs закончить fix; чек/счёт registers; ещё;
  toasts; the asking-about-people script (target #2) as a reusable
  question battery. **Stories begin**: past-tense narration through
  telling short anecdotes (target #3) — tenses re-entered through
  writing joy (5/5), never as conjugation tables (verbs joy 2/5).
- **Unit 4 · The Data Platform** (Dima's office — emphasis #2). His
  professional register: данные, платформа, архитектура; opinions
  with потому что; perfective future in plans («я позвоню», rematch
  v5q5); geopolitics-lite debates (target #1) at controlled
  vocabulary. He asked Dima's company for a job in the diagnostic —
  Unit 4's arc is the interview.
- **cp2 · Checkpoint: The Standup.**
- **Unit 5 · The Return, properly.** Conditional (пошёл бы — rematch,
  B1+ now fair game), long-form stories, full conversations on his
  three targets. Capstone: the oral protocol re-run at full ladder —
  measured against week 1.
