# Timothy's Russian — results rubric (how to steer from the diagnostic)

> For whoever (human or agent) reads the results and writes the course
> plan. Inputs: `vozvrat-diag-checkpoint-*.json` (family folder) +
> `docs/results/vozvrat-oral-*.md`. Output: a course plan per Playbook
> Step 3 (`docs/plans/vozvrat-plan.md`). Steering = senior work.
> Read `vozvrat-diagnostic.md` first for what each question is
> diagnostic of; this file is the decision procedure.

## Step 1 — Clean the marks

1. Read every `review` part's `raw` and re-mark by meaning. For the
   scored-spelling parts (grammar endings, v7q1–q2) distinguish three
   outcomes, not two: right · wrong-form · **right-form-misspelled**
   (e.g. a case ending correct but a stem typo). The third is a typing
   problem, not a grammar problem — count it as grammatically right
   and note it separately.
2. ё/е and listed leniencies are already in the accept lists; don't
   re-penalise at review.
3. Gender slips (была, пошла бы) marked correct by design — log them
   once if they recur; one line in the plan, not a unit.
4. `skipped` clusters mark band edges. One skipped question inside an
   otherwise-held band = probe it in conversation before believing it.

## Step 2 — The two headline measures

**(a) Recognition–retrieval gap**, per vocabulary strand: compare
Ru→En (v2q1–2, v3q1–2) against En→Ru (v2q3, v3q3) hit rates.
- Large gap (recognition ≥ double retrieval) = **rust profile**:
  early sessions are reactivation — high-volume recall of
  known-but-buried words. Fast course.
- Small gap with both low = **true-gap profile**: early sessions
  teach. Slower ramp, more scaffolding.

**(b) Grammar break-map**, from v4 (cases) and v5 (verbs): the first
band where each system fails —
- prep/acc (v4q1–2), gen (v4q3), dat/instr (v4q4), gen-pl (v4q5),
  agreement (v4q6);
- conjugation (v5q1), past (v5q2), motion (v5q3), aspect concept
  (v5q4 — BOTH right = intact; split = guessing), perfective future
  (v5q5), conditional (v5q6).
Cross-reference v8q4 (his own "what broke me"): where self-report and
measurement AGREE, that is Unit 1/2 material. Where he fears
something he scored fine on → confidence problem: treat with volume
and wins (usage drills in scenario), not re-teaching. Where he's
confident but scored badly → blind spot: needs explicit teaching,
gently framed.

## Step 3 — Merge the oral findings

- Scored-vs-spontaneous on the planted forms (работаю архитектором,
  жил в Москве, past narration): right in the checkpoint but absent/
  wrong at oral rung 2–3 = forms are KNOWLEDGE, not yet USABLE —
  sessions need forced-production beats, not more exercises.
- Oral comprehension score ≤2, or text-mode "LISTENING UNASSESSED" →
  the plan MUST carry a listening workstream outside the platform
  (daily Russian audio habit fitted to the 15-min budget) and say
  plainly that the platform does not cover it. Do not let the written
  score masquerade as the whole picture.
- Repair-strategy score is the best single predictor of the bar goal:
  ≤2 → every unit gets a "staying in Russian when stuck" pattern
  (asking for words in Russian, paraphrase drills). It is a trainable
  skill and the cheapest win toward "comfortable".

## Step 4 — Enjoyment and the harvest

- v8q1 vs strand scores, both directions (defer high-skill/low-joy;
  lead low-skill/high-joy). Watch cases enjoyment specifically:
  ≥3 despite breakage → he has appetite for a direct grammar assault
  (name the enemy, fight it); ≤2 → smuggle cases in through scenarios
  and never show a declension table unprompted.
- v8q3 (fond memory): whatever he names becomes course scenery — a
  real bar, a real person alongside Dima. v8q5 (three bar topics):
  these ARE the B-level syllabus targets; build topic vocabulary
  lanes toward exactly them. Expect data/architecture; don't assume.

## Step 5 — Write the course plan

Playbook Step 3, adult-adapted: ~10 weeks, ~5 units. **Weekly rhythm
must fit 15 min/day + 1 h weekend** — propose 5–6 micro-sessions/week
(one platform session split across 2–3 sittings via save/resume) +
the weekend hour for the long session and the oral/conversation work.
Checkpoints after ~unit 2 and ~unit 4 (`kind:"checkpoint"`, ids like
`cp1` — no hyphens). Rematch list: every A1–B1 miss returns as a boss
question; B1+ misses seed checkpoint 1. The plan carries "Notes for
Timothy" as supervising adult AND learner — including the standing
note that the written diagnostic's B1+ ceiling means a clean sweep
there requires re-probing at B2 before trusting any level claim.

## Hard floor

Cyrillic input stays required. ё/е leniency stays. Free writing is
never machine-marked. Session lengths respect the 15-minute sitting.
And every steering decision lands in a committed file, not a chat —
that rule is why this course's reasoning still exists.
