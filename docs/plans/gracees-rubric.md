# Grace's Spanish — results rubric (how to steer from the diagnostic)

> For whoever (human or agent) reads the results and writes the course
> plan. Inputs: the checkpoint results JSON in the family folder
> (`gracees-diag-checkpoint-*.json`) + the oral protocol sheet.
> Output: a course plan per Playbook Step 3 (`docs/plans/gracees-plan.md`).
> Steering is senior work — not a content-agent task (CONTENT_GUIDE
> review protocol).

## Step 1 — Clean the marks before believing them

The engine stores `mark` per part: `correct` / `incorrect` (numeric
only) / `review` / `skipped` / `blank`.

1. **Read every `review` part's `raw` answer and re-mark it by
   meaning.** For Grace, `review` usually means "right idea, spelling
   or phrasing off-list". Credit: meaning right = treat as correct;
   half-answers (e.g. "juice" for zumo de naranja) = half; note the
   good misses — they're teaching gold.
2. **Spelling is NEVER a miss.** If the only distance between raw and
   accept is orthography (including ñ→n, accents, doubled letters),
   it is correct. No exceptions; this is the style sheet in rubric
   form.
3. Marking artefacts: numeric parts are robust here (the engine
   normalises euros/digits), but check any `incorrect` numeric for a
   written-out word answer ("four") before believing it — that's a
   format artefact, not a wrong answer (the Playbook's "bare 50"
   lesson).
4. `skipped` is information: a skip cluster marks a band edge far
   more reliably than scattered wrong answers.

## Step 2 — Per-strand top-out

For each section, find: last band comfortable / first band of
struggle / first band out of reach. Expected beginner profile:
comfortable A0, struggle A1, out of reach A1+/A2. The course pitches
each strand at ITS struggle point (Playbook: struggle, not comfort).

Readings that change the plan:
- **s1–s2 strong (trips left real residue)** → Unit 1 skips
  greetings; opens with the cognate reveal and her own suitcase words.
- **s2q1 right, s2q2 wrong** → cognate instinct without stock: the
  ideal profile for the "you already know hundreds of words" reveal
  as Unit 1's opener.
- **s3 strong or s3 enjoyment ≥4** → run a money thread through the
  course (prices, the market stall, tipping) — her analyst identity
  imported, not duplicated from graceco.
- **s4 anything right** → she has school Spanish or sharp instinct;
  ASK HER which (and escalate the school-curriculum question to
  Timothy again — it changes sequencing, we still don't know it).
- **s5 pattern**: s5q1–q3 right with s5q4–q5 missed is healthy
  (planted words carried, unplanted ones didn't). All five right →
  reading band is A1+ already; raise the postcard difficulty in
  checkpoints.
- **Oral sheet**: Level 3 (willingness to speak) outranks every
  written number. Freeze at level 3 → Unit 1 sessions end with
  one-word speaking dares only; eager at level 3 → every session
  gets a "say it out loud" beat.

## Step 3 — Ability–enjoyment gaps (both ways)

From s6q1 ratings vs strand scores:
- high skill + low joy → defer the strand, re-enter it later through
  something she rated 5 (Playbook Step 2).
- low skill + high joy → lead with it, pitched gently.
- The survey free-texts (s6q2–q4) outrank the numbers: her own words
  decide Unit 1's scenario (food / pool-social / reading signs — or
  her fourth thing). Grace named percentages last time and it became
  Unit 1; expect her to name the unit again.

## Step 4 — The rematch list

Extract every part marked incorrect/review-not-credited/skipped at
bands A0–A1+ into a list in the course plan. These return as boss
questions in sessions, labelled as rematches ("this exact question
was on your suitcase check"). A2 misses do NOT go on the list — they
were built to be missed; they seed checkpoint 1 instead.

## Step 5 — Write the course plan

Per Playbook Step 3: ~10 weeks, ~5 units, 3 × ~35 min/week, early
wins first, checkpoints after ~unit 2 and ~unit 4 reusing
`kind:"checkpoint"`. Required in the plan: strand/priority table from
Step 2, the rematch list, the oral-protocol accommodations (speaking
asks sized to her willingness score), and "Notes for Timothy".
Sessions are then produced iteratively (1–2 at a time, watch results)
— never batch-produce; the handoff for that goes to a content agent
with CONTENT_GUIDE.md, NOT this file.

## Hard floor (do not re-litigate per result)

Whatever the results: short sentences, one idea per line, bigText
stays on, spelling never graded, skips never shamed, sessions ~35
min. These are style-sheet invariants, not tuning knobs.
